# Team Charter: Meridian Foundations

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The Foundations team (me as initiative owner, PM 1, Field Mobile, Office Web and Design) owns the three Now bets: crew time entry, the 4-field daily log and in-app photo markup. We're measured by 55% of new field users active 3+ days a week at week 8, 60% of field updates acted on by office coordinators within 24 hours, and a 35% cut in median days to approval. PM 1 keeps at least 70% of Field Mobile's sprint capacity on those bets through quarter end, and I set the order and start gates for the Next bets.
>
> **Out of scope:** PM 2 and Enterprise Sales own renewal requests and features for single accounts; Platform owns offline sync and, with Data & Insights, the multi-project data model. Growth owns push alert delivery, and Office Web and AI/ML own AI-drafted RFIs. The Later bets (sub portal, checklists, weather) have no team or PM, so Enterprise Sales can't sell them and no engineering lead can staff them this quarter.
>
> **Joint calls:** PM 1, PM 2 and I must all approve in the Jira ticket before an engineering lead moves a Field Mobile or Office Web engineer off a Now bet for more than 2 engineer-days in a sprint, before PM 2 adds a required daily log field, time entry step or photo flow for an enterprise account, and before anyone changes a Now target. PM 1 and a Data & Insights analyst confirm each week-4 Now signal before Platform, Growth or AI/ML start a Next bet, and the Platform lead approves any new data field from the Now bets before Field Mobile ships it.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** PM 1 makes feature and scope calls for time entry, the daily log and photo markup within Field Mobile's 70% floor, and PM 2 makes them for enterprise work within the other 30%. The Field Mobile and Office Web engineering leads decide how to build and own the estimates, and the Design lead decides which 4 daily log fields are required. Enterprise Sales sends requests only to PM 2, and it cannot assign work to an engineer or promise a customer a date.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** The PM or engineering lead who hits the conflict opens a Jira ticket within 1 business day and posts it in the Foundations Slack channel. The ticket states the ask, the engineer-days, which KR slips and by how many weeks, and the renewal's annual revenue and date. If PM 1 and PM 2 can't settle it in 2 business days, I make the final call as Foundations owner within 2 more, and Field Mobile stays on the field bet until I decide; only a call that would break the 70% floor goes to the VP of Product and the CRO, who decide within 3 business days.
